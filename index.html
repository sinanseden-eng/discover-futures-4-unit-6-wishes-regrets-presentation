Moving Day: The Inner Monologue

Moving to a new home forces us to reflect. We wish things were different, we regret what we left behind, and we imagine how things could have been. Click the panels below to decode the grammar of our thoughts.

Concept 1

Wishes about the Present

Wanting a current situation to be different (but it's impossible or unlikely).

Wish / If only + Past Simple

Contextual Dialogue: The Freezing Flat

Ali and Bora just moved into a small, old apartment in Istanbul in January.

Ali: "This new flat is freezing, and there is barely enough room for my bed."

Bora: "I know. I wish this place had central heating instead of a tiny stove."

Ali: "If only it were a bit bigger, too. We can't even fit a sofa in the living room!"

Common Error

Using the Present Tense to talk about a present wish.

I wish I have a bigger room.

I wish I had a bigger room.

Concept 2

Regrets about the Past

Expressing sadness or regret about something that happened (or didn't happen) before now.

Wish / If only + Past Perfect (had + V3)

Mini-Story: The Fragile Box

Zeynep tried to pack all her kitchen supplies quickly before the movers arrived. When she opened the box in her new house, all her grandmother's tea glasses were broken. She looked at the mess and sighed, "I wish I had wrapped them in bubble wrap." Her mother shook her head, "If only you hadn't rushed the packing process last night."

Common Error

Using the Past Simple instead of Past Perfect for past regrets.

I wish I didn't leave my keys yesterday.

I wish I hadn't left my keys yesterday.

Concept 3

Complaints & Habits

Expressing annoyance about someone's current behavior or habits that you want them to stop.

Wish / If only + Would + Base Verb

Contextual Dialogue: The Messy Roommate

Mert: "Why are there empty pizza boxes all over the living room again?"

Can: "I'll clean them up later, just relax."

Mert: "I wish you would stop leaving your trash everywhere! It's a shared house."

Can: "And if only you wouldn't complain every single morning!"

Common Error

Using "would" to talk about yourself or about a state instead of an action.

I wish I would speak better English.

I wish I spoke better English. (Because it's about yourself!)

Concept 4

Past Consequences

Imagining a different past action and the different consequence it would have caused (Third Conditional).

If + Past Perfect (had + V3),
Subject + would have + V3

Mini-Story: The Broken TV

The moving company offered insurance, but Emre declined it to save money. During the drive, the truck hit a massive pothole and his expensive TV shattered. "If I had bought the insurance, they would have paid for a new TV," he groaned, holding his head. "I wouldn't have lost so much money if I hadn't been so cheap."

Common Error

Putting "would have" inside the If-clause.

If I would have packed it, I wouldn't have lost it.

If I had packed it, I wouldn't have lost it.

The Nomad Simulation

You decided to sell your permanent home and live out of a van as a modern digital nomad. Apply your grammar knowledge to deal with the harsh realities of the road!

🚐🌧️ Scenario 1

The roof of your van is leaking right onto your bed. You wish the rain would stop, but the forecast says it will rain all week.

Formulate a Complaint (Wish + would):

I wish the rain...

Hint: Think about what you want the weather to do (stop).

🎸🚮 Scenario 2

Before you left, you cleared out your room and gave away your acoustic guitar to save space. Now you're lonely around the campfire and regret it.

Formulate a Past Regret (If only + Past Perfect):

If only I...

Hint: Express regret using the negative form of "give away" + "my guitar".

🥶🗺️ Scenario 3

You decided to drive north into the mountains instead of south to the beach. Now your engine is frozen and you are stuck.

Formulate a Past Consequence (3rd Conditional):

If we had driven south, the engine...

Hint: What wouldn't have happened? Use the verb "freeze".

💻📵 Scenario 4

You found a breathtaking cliffside parking spot, but you can't submit your project because there is zero cell service.

Formulate a Present Wish (Wish + Past Simple):

I wish I...

Hint: State what you lack right now (verb: "have" + "a signal").

☀️🔋 Scenario 5

You decided to save money and didn't install solar panels. Now your van battery is completely dead.

Formulate a Past Consequence (3rd Conditional):

If we had installed solar panels, the battery...

Hint: What wouldn't have happened? Use the verb "die".

🚐🔊 Scenario 6

A massive RV parked right next to you and their loud generator has been running for 4 hours.

Formulate a Complaint (If only + would):

If only they...

Hint: State the action you want them to take ("turn off" + "the generator").

🧥📦 Scenario 7

You hoarded five heavy winter coats just in case. Now you have no room for groceries in the tiny closet.

Formulate a Past Regret (Wish + Past Perfect):

I wish I...

Hint: Express regret about the action ("pack" + "so many coats").

🐕🚐 Scenario 8

Living in a 5-meter van with a large, energetic dog is starting to feel very cramped and uncomfortable.

Formulate a Present Wish (If only + Past Simple):

If only the van...

Hint: Express a wish about the van's size using the verb "be" + "bigger".

🦝🍔 Scenario 9

You left the side door open to enjoy the breeze, and a raccoon ran in and stole your only bag of chips.

Formulate a Past Consequence (3rd Conditional):

If I hadn't left the door open, the raccoon...

Hint: What wouldn't have happened? Use the verb "steal" + "my chips".
