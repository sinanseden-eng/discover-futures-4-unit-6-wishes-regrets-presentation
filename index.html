Moving Day: The Inner Monologue

Moving to a new home forces us to reflect. We wish things were different, we regret what we left behind, and we imagine how things could have been. Click the panels below to decode the grammar of our thoughts.

Wish / If only + Past Simple

Contextual Examples:

  • "I wish this new apartment felt more cozy."

    (Reality: It doesn't feel cozy right now.)

  • "If only we had a permanent place to stay."

    (Reality: We do not have a permanent place.)

Concept 2

Regrets about the Past

Expressing sadness or regret about something that happened (or didn't happen) before now.

Wish / If only + Past Perfect (had + V3)

Contextual Examples:

  • "I wish I had cleared out my closet before packing."

    (Reality: I didn't clear it out, and now I have too many boxes.)

  • "If only we hadn't moved away from our friends."

    (Reality: We did move away, and I regret it.)

Concept 3

Complaints & Habits

Expressing annoyance about someone's current behavior or habits that you want them to stop.

Wish / If only + Would + Base Verb

Note: We cannot use "wish + would" to talk about ourselves (You can't say: I wish I would...)

Contextual Examples:

  • "I wish my brother wouldn't hoard so much junk in his room."

    (Reality: He constantly hoards things, and it annoys me.)

  • "If only the movers would be more careful with the fragile boxes!"

    (Reality: They are being careless right now.)

Concept 4

Past Consequences

Imagining a different past action and the different consequence it would have caused (Third Conditional).

If + Past Perfect (had + V3),
Subject + would have + V3

Contextual Examples:

  • "If we had stayed in our old house, we would have felt a greater sense of belonging."

    (Reality: We didn't stay, so we didn't feel that belonging.)

  • "I wouldn't have lost my favorite book if I had packed it away properly."

    (Reality: I didn't pack it properly, so the consequence is that I lost it.)

Training Exercises

Test your understanding of the 4 grammar rules. Select the correct structure to complete the thought.

Rule 1: Present Wishes

"My new room is so small and dark."

I wish my new room ________________ bigger windows.

Rule 2: Past Regrets

"I didn't bring my thick winter coat, and now I'm freezing."

I wish I ________________ my winter coat.

Rule 3: Complaints

"My roommate constantly leaves empty boxes in the hallway."

If only he ________________ leaving his boxes everywhere!

Rule 4: Past Consequences

"Because we threw away the old map, we got completely lost."

If we hadn't thrown away the map, we ________________ lost.

The Nomad Simulation

You decided to sell your permanent home and live out of a van as a modern digital nomad. Apply your grammar knowledge to deal with the harsh realities of the road!

🚐🌧️ Scenario 1

The roof of your van is leaking right onto your bed. You wish the rain would stop, but the forecast says it will rain all week.

Formulate a Complaint (Wish + would):

I wish the rain...
🎸🚮 Scenario 2

Before you left, you cleared out your room and gave away your acoustic guitar to save space. Now you're lonely around the campfire and regret it.

Formulate a Past Regret (If only + Past Perfect):

If only I...

Hint: Use the words "hadn't given away my guitar".

🥶🗺️ Scenario 3

You decided to drive north into the mountains instead of south to the beach. Now your engine is frozen and you are stuck.

Formulate a Past Consequence (3rd Conditional):

If we had driven south, the engine...

Hint: Use the words "wouldn't have frozen".

💻📵 Scenario 4

You found a breathtaking cliffside parking spot, but you can't submit your project because there is zero cell service.

Formulate a Present Wish (Wish + Past Simple):

I wish I...

Hint: Use the words "had a signal".

☀️🔋 Scenario 5

You decided to save money and didn't install solar panels. Now your van battery is completely dead.

Formulate a Past Consequence (3rd Conditional):

If we had installed solar panels, the battery...

Hint: Use the words "wouldn't have died".

🚐🔊 Scenario 6

A massive RV parked right next to you and their loud generator has been running for 4 hours.

Formulate a Complaint (If only + would):

If only they...

Hint: Use the words "would turn off the generator".

🧥📦 Scenario 7

You hoarded five heavy winter coats just in case. Now you have no room for groceries in the tiny closet.

Formulate a Past Regret (Wish + Past Perfect):

I wish I...

Hint: Use the words "hadn't packed so many coats".

🐕🚐 Scenario 8

Living in a 5-meter van with a large, energetic dog is starting to feel very cramped and uncomfortable.

Formulate a Present Wish (If only + Past Simple):

If only the van...

Hint: Use the words "were bigger".

🦝🍔 Scenario 9

You left the side door open to enjoy the breeze, and a raccoon ran in and stole your only bag of chips.

Formulate a Past Consequence (3rd Conditional):

If I hadn't left the door open, the raccoon...

Hint: Use the words "wouldn't have stolen my chips".
